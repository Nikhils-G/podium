Embed the gallery

Paste this where you want the projects to appear. Add ?theme=dark or &track=trk_id to the URL.

<iframe src="{{ base_url }}/e/{{ event.slug }}/embed" width="100%" height="600" loading="lazy" title="{{ event.name }} projects"></iframe>

Preview the embed ↗ · JSON for custom widgets: GET /api/v1/events/{{ event.slug }}/projects (CORS enabled).
