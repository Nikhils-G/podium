Ranking

Disagreement is the spread (standard deviation) of the normalized scores a project received: 0 means every judge agreed, above 1 means judges saw it very differently and it deserves a look.

Confidence comes from a bootstrap: 300 times, each project's own reviews are re-drawn with replacement and the field is re-ranked, with judge calibration held fixed. "72% first" means the project came first in 72% of those re-draws; the expected rank is its average. A project with a single review can't be re-drawn, so it shows no estimate — its score still counts as a fixed point for the others.

Judge calibration
