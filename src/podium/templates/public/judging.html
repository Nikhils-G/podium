What normalization did

{% if normalized %}

Judges differ in how generously they score. Each judge's scores were standardised against their own mean and spread (with shrinkage k = {{ '%g'|format(results.k) }} towards the event-wide mean {{ '%.1f'|format(results.global_mean) }} and spread {{ '%.1f'|format(results.global_std) }}, so judges with few reviews are not over-corrected). A judge who scored every project identically contributed no ranking information. The published ranking uses these normalized scores.

How sure is the ranking?

A bootstrap re-drew every project's reviews {{ (confidence.values()|first).samples if confidence else 300 }} times and re-ranked the field each time. The share of re-draws in which a project kept its medal is shown on the results page; a rank that survives most re-draws is robust, a coin flip between two neighbours is exactly that.
